01 / The artist

THIS IS WREAL X.

A visual home for the music, story, releases, videos and the people behind the numbers.

Wreal X is a South Sudanese artist whose work leans into emotional storytelling, melody and trap-inspired production. The website is designed as a living artist profile rather than a static bio page.

As the artist catalogue grows, this space can grow with it — releases, videos, milestones, streaming destinations, social links, merchandise and fan updates all in one place.
