WREAL X

born Angel Jambu Roy · July 20, 2007 · Yei, South Sudan

Wreal X is a South Sudanese singer, emo rapper, and songwriter from East Africa. Deeply influenced by Juice WRLD, he masterfully blends the raw vulnerability of emo trap with the rhythmic warmth of Afrobeats — creating a sound that is both haunting and deeply resonant. His music navigates the blues, romance, love, and the harsh realities of life — each track a confession, every verse a glimpse into a soul marked by both suffering and hope.

Born to the late Mr. Taban Roy and Mrs. Santuke Koloy Kamba, Wreal X discovered his passion for music at just ten years old. He began writing rap songs as a child, pouring his emotions into lyrics long before he understood the weight of his own words. As he grew, so did his understanding of the industry and the hip-hop world, leading him to emo trap — a genre that gave voice to his pain. Through the influence of Juice WRLD, he found his true artistic identity.

"Every verse is a confession — a soul marked by suffering and hope."

His early releases, "Monalisa" and "Photo", carried Afro influences familiar to his homeland, but it was "Hurry Up" — his first trap record — that announced his arrival, surpassing one thousand streams on YouTube. Its successor, "Gang Gang", cemented his identity as a fully realized emo trap artisan: audacious in production, unflinching in lyrical candor, and utterly unlike anything emerging from his region.

Behind the music lies a young man who has faced familial opposition, institutional disillusionment, and the isolation of pioneering an unrecognized genre in a land rooted in local sounds. He has walked through broken promises from industry figures who deemed his vision too foreign, too perilous.
