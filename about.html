ABOUT

CURSED

Wreal X is a South Sudanese singer, emo rapper, and songwriter from East Africa.

Deeply influenced by Juice WRLD, his music blends the emotional intensity of emo trap with the warmth of Afrobeats.

Every record carries the ache of love, loss, and survival — honest, raw, and unforgettable.
