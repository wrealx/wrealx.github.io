About Wreal X

Wreal X is a multidisciplinary artist moving through sound, emotion, and the kind of truth that feels less like a statement and more like a confession.

Built on raw honesty, cinematic melodies, and restless energy, Wreal X blends intimate storytelling with a bold, modern pulse.

The project is rooted in the feeling of being both cursed and alive — torn between shadow and clarity, memory and motion.
