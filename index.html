"He believes he is cursed. But listen closely, and you'll hear a young man slowly unbuilding that curse — one lyric, one track, one dream at a time."

Angel Jambu Roy was born July 20, 2007, in Yei, Central Equatoria State — a small town where rhythm and melody became the only language that offered him solace. While the sounds around him leaned Afro, he was drawn to emo trap: artists who turned darkness into art.

In 2020, his father's death upended everything, pulling him back to a homeland that felt both familiar and foreign. That loss, layered onto a family unraveling, planted the belief that he carried a curse. In the studio, that same weight became fuel.

His earliest releases, Monalisa and Photo, still carried the Afro sound of home. Then came Hurry Up — his first trap record, and the moment Wreal X announced himself as something else entirely.

He's faced disillusionment, isolation, and broken promises from people who called his vision too risky. He kept building anyway — engineering a corridor between South Sudan and global emo trap, track by track.
